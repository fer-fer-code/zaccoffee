Our Story

ZACCOFFEE began with a love for hospitality and the connections made over a cup of coffee.

After five years working in hospitality in the UAE, our founder, Ibrahim Muzuula, returned to Uganda with a desire to build something of his own. His experience taught him that memorable moments come from thoughtful service, attention to detail, and making people feel welcome.

That spirit became the foundation of ZACCOFFEE.

Founded in Uganda, we are preparing a range of whole bean and ground coffee sourced from Uganda and Indonesia. Our vision is to bring these distinct origins to your everyday coffee ritual, with care in how we select, present, and share each product.

We are at the beginning of our journey, building a brand rooted in quality, warm hospitality, and pride in where we come from.

Our mission

To cultivate and source quality coffee, offer thoughtfully packaged products, and create welcoming coffee experiences through professional barista services.

Our vision

Our vision is to build ZACCOFFEE into a trusted Ugandan coffee brand with an international reach, sharing the distinct flavours of coffee from Uganda and Indonesia while bringing people together through quality coffee and warm hospitality.

Quality, honesty, care, and connection.

Our promise

Clear product information, careful packaging, and friendly, reliable service.
